WHAT THIS RELEASE HOLDS

BEFORE YOU FLASH

Which cable goes where

The master is the board with the screen. Its USB-C socket is all you need for the master itself.

Each sensor has a USB-C socket of its own. While you flash a sensor through its own socket, keep the cell or the master's USB plugged in as well: a cable in the sensor alone starves the rest of the unit.

What an update keeps

An update writes the bootloader, the partition table and the application. The master's settings, its paired phones and the signals it has learned sit elsewhere in its flash and are left alone, unless you tick the box that erases them.

If a board does not answer

Unplug its USB cable, hold its BOOT button, plug the cable in, and let go. The board then waits in its loader, and the page can write to it from there. A flash that was cut off is mended the same way.

What the page needs

A browser with Web Serial on a computer: Chrome and Edge have it. Close other programs that hold the board's port, such as a serial monitor.

The page talks to the board on your USB port and fetches the firmware from this site. It sends nothing anywhere else.

The detector only listens for aircraft and their controllers. Its sensors never transmit, and the master's one radio link is Bluetooth to a phone.
