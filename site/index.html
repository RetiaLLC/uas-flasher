WHAT THIS RELEASE HOLDS

BEFORE YOU FLASH

Which cable goes where

To flash the master, the board with the screen, connect the cable to the master's USB-C port.

To flash a sensor through its own USB-C port, keep the battery or the master's USB cable connected too. A cable in the sensor alone doesn't supply enough power for the rest of the unit.

What an update keeps

An update keeps the master's settings, its paired phones, and the signals it has learned. To erase them, select the erase option on the master's card before you flash.

The first time you flash a new board, its settings area is erased.

If a board doesn't respond

Disconnect its USB cable. Hold the BOOT button, reconnect the cable, and then release the button. The board starts in its loader, and you can flash it again. This also recovers a board after an interrupted flash.

What you need

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones and tablets can't use Web Serial.

Close other programs that use the board's port, such as a serial monitor.

The page downloads firmware from this site only and sends nothing to other sites.

The detector listens for aircraft and their controllers. The sensors don't transmit. The master's only radio link is Bluetooth to your phone.
